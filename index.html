File404 Upload

A personal desktop tool that schedules uploads of the File404 channel's own videos to YouTube.

Uses YouTube API Services

File404 Upload uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, File404, and to schedule when YouTube makes them public. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Lists finished videos on the creator's computer.
  • Shows a preview first: title, scheduled time, "made for kids: No", altered or synthetic content disclosure.
  • Uploads only after the owner confirms. Videos are uploaded as private with a scheduled publish time.
  • Optionally sets the thumbnail for long videos.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the File404 YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users, and it does not access any other channel.
