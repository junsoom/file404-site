Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

YouTube API Services

File404 Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using File404 Upload you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Please also see the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

1. Who we are

File404 Upload is a personal tool run by the owner of the File404 YouTube channel. It runs only on the owner's own computer. There is no server, no website account and no other users.

2. What data we access

With your permission (Google sign-in), File404 Upload uses these YouTube API scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload your videos and set thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly — only to read your channel name, so you can confirm the right channel is connected.

It does not read your viewers' data, comments, analytics, subscribers, playlists or any other channel.

3. How we use the data

  • To upload the videos you choose, with the title, description, tags and scheduled publish time you review first.
  • To show you the connected channel name and the link of each uploaded video.

4. How we store the data

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your own computer, in a private file (.secrets/youtube_token.json, readable only by your user account).
  • A local upload log keeps the video ID, title and scheduled time of each upload, to avoid uploading the same video twice.
  • Nothing is stored on any server operated by us.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, through the YouTube API, to perform the upload. If you use the optional Telegram bot, your commands and the upload status messages pass through Telegram's servers; no Google token is ever sent to Telegram.

6. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Delete the local files .secrets/youtube_token.json and the upload log at any time. This removes all stored data.
  • You can revoke File404 Upload's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • Ask us to delete anything by email; we will reply within 7 days.

7. Cookies and tracking

File404 Upload does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. This website does not use cookies either.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
